title: "17. RVX Miner Community Arcade"
---

RVX Miner is a playable community arcade game built around short mining rounds, persistent progression and verified competition. Its purpose is to give the RAVIXO community an accessible activity alongside market research, demo trading and learning. Arcade points and achievements are recorded separately from Earned RVX Allocations and on-chain RVX.

The game can be tested independently. Version 17 supports a separate ledger for real USD Hunt rewards and funded daily budget limits, and lets you continue mining after completing a Campaign or Endless objective. RVX ID integration, game-related referral attribution and automatic external payment processing remain implementation steps. Hunt opens only after a dated prize fund is allocated and its eligibility and payment terms are published. Availability in the main RAVIXO application will be identified separately.

## Game walkthrough

[Explore the RVX Miner game flow and visual explanation](https://notebooklm.link.google/0rBPwWqhZOhF). Use this companion walkthrough to picture the mining journey, progression and reward hunt. The rules and funded campaign terms below govern eligibility and real USD rewards.

In brief: choose a mode and your equipment, mine within the round timer, deliver items to complete objectives, and use earned resources to progress. In Hunt, deliver two distinct keys, then bring the unlocked gift to the surface to qualify for a real USD reward of USD 0.05-10.00, subject to verification and available campaign funding.

## 17.1. The Core Game Loop

Players choose an unlocked stage, one character and one tool, then enter a round lasting at most 60 seconds. The hook swings automatically; the player chooses when to release it. There is no aiming guide or automatic targeting. Rocks, bombs and selected objective items move to create timing challenges.

Only items delivered to the surface count toward resources and objectives. Touching an item or carrying it when time expires does not complete a collection. A bomb normally removes three seconds and drops the carried item back into the mine. Required objective items are not permanently removed by an explosion.

In Campaign and Endless Mine, completing the delivery objective does not end your round. You can keep mining until the countdown reaches zero. Bomb penalties still reduce the remaining time, including after you complete the objective. The game records your first objective completion time for the speed star and awards the time bonus only once. Stars, stage unlocks and collected resources are confirmed when the round ends.

For example, a stage requiring one Mine RVX collectible and one ore item remains playable after you deliver both. If 41 seconds remain, you can use that time to collect more resources.

Treasure objectives require two distinct keys to be delivered before you can bring the gift or treasure wagon to the surface. In Practice, Hunt and Ranked, delivering the unlocked gift ends the round early; collecting ordinary Mine RVX does not. After an early gift delivery, the timer freezes at the actual remaining time instead of resetting to 00:00. Delivered resources remain recorded after an unsuccessful attempt.

## 17.2. Campaign Map and Play Modes

The current campaign contains three chapters, 18 regions and 180 sequential stages, with up to 540 stars. Each region presents a winding trail of ten stage nodes. Objectives include Mine RVX collectibles, gems, ore containing parts, keys, a moving rescue drone and a moving treasure wagon.

| Chapter | Stages | Progression focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | 1-60 | Introduces timing and collection before combining objectives |
| Depths | 61-120 | Adds drifting RVX collectibles and more combined objectives |
| Legends | 121-180 | Uses harder combinations and a second moving gate for the final wagon |

Clearing a stage opens the next stage in its region. Opening the next region requires at least 18 of the previous region's 30 stars and completion of its final wagon stage. The map uses 30 objective templates across regional variants and server-generated layouts; the 180 stages are not 180 entirely different rule sets.

Completing the objective earns one star. Avoiding all bombs throughout the entire round adds a second. Completing the objective for the first time with at least the displayed time remaining adds a third. Continuing to mine does not remove your speed star, but a later bomb hit removes eligibility for the no-bomb star. Only your best stage result is retained for campaign stars. Replays can improve that result without repeating the first-clear reward.

| Mode | Purpose | Current limit or rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign | Clear stages, earn stars and unlock regions | At most 60 seconds; keep mining after completing the objective |
| Endless Mine | Complete successive objectives and preserve cleared depth | Opens after the first completed region; keep mining after completing the objective, with depth confirmed at round end; cycles the stage catalogue with new layouts and bounded difficulty |
| Practice | Learn timing and earn internal resources | Includes a one-time active-practice tutorial reward |
| Hunt | Deliver two keys and the gift to earn USD 0.05-10.00 in real rewards | Three starts per account per day; at most one USD gift per day while a funded campaign is open |
| Ranked | Compete using standardized equipment | Ten starts per day; the five best daily scores contribute to its weekly standings |

Campaign, Endless Mine and Practice allow limited pausing. Hunt and Ranked clocks continue when the player leaves the game view. Arcade day and week boundaries use UTC+7.

## 17.3. Internal Resources and Milestones

| Record | How it is earned | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mine RVX | Delivered RVX collectibles, quests and eligible progression milestones | Unlocks and upgrades game equipment; it is not a wallet token |
| Parts | Delivered ore containing parts and eligible milestone rewards | Pays equipment unlock and upgrade costs |
| Mastery XP | Delivered items and completed objectives using the equipped character and tool | Qualifies that character and tool for later levels |
| Stage stars | Objective completion, avoiding bombs and the displayed time threshold | Opens region gates and star chests |
| Relics | First clears of fixed stages 2, 4, 7 and 10 in each region | Completes 18 collection sets containing 72 relic entries |
| Season points and medals | Eligible Campaign and Endless victories | Unlocks current-season milestones and records long-term achievement |

The base mining value is 10 Mine RVX per delivered RVX collectible. Equipment can improve internal earnings within a total 30% bonus cap. A delivered ore item containing a part awards one part. Mining, the tutorial and daily contracts share a daily allowance of 500 Mine RVX and three parts. Fixed first-clear, star-chest, completed-set, prestige, seasonal and weekly-race awards have separate allowances outside that mining cap.

A first stage clear grants 20 Mine RVX; challenge stages 5 and 10 also grant one part. Region star chests at 6, 12, 18, 24 and 30 stars grant 40, 60, 80, 100 and 120 Mine RVX respectively, with 0, 1, 1, 1 and 2 parts. Completing a four-relic regional set grants 120 Mine RVX and two parts. These milestones are awarded once per eligible stage, chest or set.

Gems contribute score or stage objectives. They are not withdrawable diamonds or a direct cash balance. Keys are round objectives, not stored currency. Existing profile XP and legacy collection records remain separate from character and tool mastery.

## 17.4. Characters Tools and Upgrades

Players can choose among six characters and six tools, producing 36 combinations. Characters support up to 20 levels and tools up to ten. Mira, Kade, Scout and Talon are initially available. Other items unlock with earned Mine RVX and parts.

Each character and tool has an advantage and a persistent drawback. Roles include wider catching, faster pulling, ore handling, first-bomb protection, additional Mine RVX and slower swing timing. An upgrade improves the disclosed benefit within stat limits; it does not remove every trade-off.

Level upgrades require both cumulative mastery XP and the listed resource cost. XP is not spent. As initial examples, character level 2 requires ten mastery XP and 35 Mine RVX; tool level 2 requires 12 mastery XP and 30 Mine RVX. Neither first upgrade requires a part. Mastery is credited to the character and tool equipped at round start, with a maximum of 30 XP for each per eligible round.

| Workshop branch | Benefit per level | Five-level limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Precision Hook | Catch radius increases by one pixel | Five additional pixels before the overall stat cap |
| B Winch Motor | Pulling modifier increases by 2% | A 10% modifier before the overall stat cap |
| C Bomb Guard | First-bomb time penalty falls by 0.2 seconds | One-second reduction before the overall protection cap |
| D Mining Storage | Mine RVX earnings modifier increases by 2% | A 10% modifier before the overall earnings cap |

The five successive branch levels cost 100, 200, 400, 700 and 1,100 Mine RVX, together with 1, 2, 3, 5 and 8 parts. Equipment is fixed when a round starts; changes affect the following round. Ranked play normalizes all character, tool and workshop stat benefits, while preserving appearance and mastery progression. Upgrades do not increase the USD value of a gift or a player's Hunt quota.

## 17.5. Earned Prestige and Seasons

Arcade prestige titles are earned through achievements. They are not purchased VIP memberships and do not activate platform fee tiers. Rewards include internal resources, profile frames, equipment presets and cosmetic hooks.

| Title | Current requirement | One-time internal reward | Additional unlock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | Clear three distinct stages | 60 Mine RVX and one part | Bronze frame |
| Silver | Complete the first region | 150 Mine RVX and two parts | Silver frame and a second equipment preset |
| Gold | Complete the first three regions and level two characters to 10 | 300 Mine RVX and three parts | Gold frame and a third preset |
| Diamond | Complete the six Discovery regions and level four characters to 15 | 600 Mine RVX and four parts | Diamond frame and a crystal hook appearance |
| Legend | Earn 180 stars, level all six characters to 20 and earn three season medals | 1,000 Mine RVX and six parts | Legend frame and an astral hook appearance |

The Legend threshold remains 180 stars even though the expanded map supports 540 stars. Completing a region means reaching 18 stars and clearing its final wagon. Cosmetic choices do not change gameplay stats or gift values.

An arcade season lasts 28 days. Campaign and Endless victories award one to three season points according to stars, capped at 15 points per day. Current milestones at 10, 25 and 40 points grant 60, 100 and 200 Mine RVX, plus one, one and two parts. Reaching 40 points records a season medal. Equipment levels, relics, earned titles and medals persist when season points reset.

## 17.6. Weekly Journey Race

The Journey Race is separate from standardized Ranked play. It uses authenticated, server-confirmed Campaign results. For each player and stage, only the best eligible result of that week counts. Ranking compares total stars, distinct cleared stages, total spare seconds and the time at which those results were achieved. Spare seconds are recorded when you first complete the objective, even though you can continue mining until the countdown expires.

The week starts on Monday at 00:00 UTC+7. A round belongs to the week in which the server started it. Results must be confirmed before 00:05 the following Monday to affect that closed week. Guest play, the test room, unsuccessful rounds and other modes do not enter this leaderboard.

| Weekly position | Mine RVX per player | Parts per player |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 250 | 3 |
| 2-3 | 180 | 2 |
| 4-10 | 100 | 1 |
| 11-20 | 50 | 0 |

These awards total at most 1,810 Mine RVX and 14 parts per week if all 20 positions qualify. Eligible players claim once during the following week after the confirmation cutoff. No USD or transferable RVX is credited by this race. Journey Race awards do not use the separate Hunt prize fund.

## 17.7. Real USD rewards and funded campaign limits

Hunt awards real USD from an allocated prize fund. To earn a gift, you must deliver two distinct keys in the same round, then bring the gift to the surface before the 60-second limit. Catching the gift or collecting the keys alone does not earn USD. Campaign, Endless Mine, Practice, Ranked, prestige titles and the Journey Race continue to award their disclosed internal resources, scores and achievements.

You have three Hunt starts per account per day and can earn at most one successful USD gift per day. A start consumes an attempt once the server accepts the round and reserves its funding. A failed round or a closed browser does not refund that attempt. A round crossing midnight uses the quota and prize fund of its start date, in UTC+7.

### Gift values and probabilities

The server randomly selects any whole-cent amount from USD 0.05 through USD 10.00, inclusive. All 996 values are possible, including USD 0.07, USD 0.13, USD 0.46 and USD 10.00. These are probability bands, not five fixed denominations.

| Gift value range | Probability of the range |
| --- | --- |
| USD 0.05-0.10 | 90% |
| USD 0.11-0.20 | 7% |
| USD 0.21-0.30 | 2% |
| USD 0.31-0.40 | 0.8% |
| USD 0.41-10.00 | 0.2% |

Each individual cent value within a band has equal probability. Small rewards remain the most common: 90% of successful gifts fall between USD 0.05 and USD 0.10. The rare USD 0.41-10.00 band contains 960 possible cent values. The individual USD 10.00 outcome therefore has an approximately 0.0002083% probability per successful gift. The theoretical mean is USD 0.0967 per successful gift. These percentages describe the value of an earned gift, not the probability of winning a round or receiving an easy layout. Character levels, tools, workshop upgrades, titles and referrals do not increase gift values or these probabilities.

### Daily prize budget

The initial planned prize allocation is USD 50 per day. RAVIXO can raise it to USD 100 per day after allocating the additional funds. The funded amount is a cap on prize obligations, not a fixed number of boxes or a promise that the full budget will be distributed each day. Payment-provider costs, if applicable, must be disclosed separately in the campaign terms.

Four cumulative budget releases open at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 UTC+7: 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of that day's funded prize budget. Before accepting a Hunt, the server reserves the maximum possible gift of USD 10.00 and privately samples its value. A confirmed win records only that value as earned USD and releases the unused reservation. A loss releases the entire USD 10.00 reservation.

A new Hunt does not start if its fund is unopened, closed or has less than USD 10.00 of released, uncommitted money available. A rejected start consumes no attempt. Existing accepted rounds settle against their original fund, and closing new entry does not cancel earned rewards. Reserving the maximum keeps all 996 gift values available at the stated probabilities; up to USD 9.99 may remain unused at the end of a day. Earned rewards plus active reservations cannot exceed the allocated budget.

### Recording and payment

A server-confirmed Hunt award is a real USD amount owed to the eligible player. The game records it in a separate reward ledger. The displayed total is cumulative USD earned; it does not by itself confirm a completed transfer or represent a current withdrawal balance. An award is marked paid only after an actual external payment is confirmed.

Automatic external payment processing and linkage to RAVIXO payment destinations are not yet integrated. Before opening a funded campaign, RAVIXO must publish its eligibility checks, payment destination requirements, payment schedule, fees and claim terms. An unopened fund issues no USD awards. Legacy simulated USD balances remain separate and do not convert into real money.

Mine RVX, parts, mastery, stars, relics and prestige retain their internal game uses. They have no automatic conversion into Earned RVX Allocations, transferable RVX, USD or stablecoins.

## 17.8. Account Verification and Community Growth

Guest progress and the quick test room are device-local demonstrations. Their sample resources and results cannot be imported into authenticated balances, community rankings or funded Hunt awards. Guests cannot enter real USD Hunt rounds. You must sign in and meet the campaign's published verification requirements to participate. The three-start and one-gift limits apply per account; verification across multiple controlled accounts requires RAVIXO identity integration. The test room previews progression and equipment without qualifying for a cash reward.

The authenticated game server determines layouts, time, collisions, objectives, scores, equipment costs and awards. Duplicate settlement and duplicate milestone claims do not create a second award. These controls reduce forged-result abuse; they do not guarantee that automated valid inputs or multiple controlled accounts will always be detected.

Optional invitation and community challenges are planned to connect arcade participation with RAVIXO onboarding. They require RVX ID and referral-attribution integration before activation. A future Campaign may recognize verified multi-day arcade activity under its published QAU criteria; the current prototype does not automatically establish QAU D7, Live qualification or a referral commission.

The existing Referral and Creator framework continues to govern attribution and acquired-user awards. A single eligible acquisition must not be paid repeatedly through game invitations, Social Share and Creator attribution. Game participation alone does not generate collected Live platform fees. The current game does not require a referral to play, upgrade equipment or unlock its ordinary treasure objectives.
