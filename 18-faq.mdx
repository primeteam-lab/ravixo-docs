---
title: "Appendix B. Frequently Asked Questions"
---

## Can I withdraw dUSDC or dUSDT?

No. Demo balances are simulated units and cannot be converted into real stablecoins.

## Is an RVX Allocation already a wallet token?

No. It is a verified entitlement recorded during the pre-TGE accumulation phase. Eligible verified units convert 1:1 into RVX at distribution.

## When will airdrop and presale distribution terms be announced?

These terms are being finalized and are not announced at this stage. Presale terms will be published before participants make payment. Airdrop distribution terms will be announced before the applicable claim or conversion process begins.

## Does RAVIXO hold the assets in my current DEX wallet?

The current DEX model is self-custodial. Users keep wallet keys and sign the supported transactions themselves. Assets used in an approved transaction follow the relevant on-chain execution.

## Are all whitepaper features available now?

No. RAVIXO opens features progressively. Check the application's feature status and official announcements.

## Do referrals earn stablecoins simply for registering users?

No. Stablecoin fee commissions require an opened program, eligible collected Live platform fees and the referrer's qualification. Fixed RVX acquisition milestones are a separate allocation program.

## Is every reward figure automatically payable?

No. A reward requires an active published Campaign or Season, completion of its conditions, verification and an available reserved budget. Planned reward schedules become active only through the published program terms.

## When will RVX and the dedicated blockchain launch?

The launch schedule will be announced through official RAVIXO channels when the network and distribution requirements are met.

## Can Mine RVX convert 1:1 into RVX?

No. Mine RVX is an internal game resource. The 1:1 rule applies only to eligible verified Earned RVX Allocations in the allocation ledger.

## Can I withdraw an RVX Miner demo gift?

No. Current USD gift records are simulated. A real payout requires a separately activated and funded Campaign with published eligibility and settlement terms.

## Does the USD 0.05-0.50 label include every cent value?

No. The current demo uses USD 0.05, 0.10, 0.20 and 0.50 slots. Future funded Campaign terms must publish their own value schedule.

## Does an arcade VIP title provide trading fee discounts?

No. Arcade prestige grants the disclosed game resources, cosmetics and equipment presets. It does not activate a platform trading fee tier.

## Do game invitations already earn referral commissions?

No. RVX ID and game referral attribution have not yet been integrated into the prototype. Stablecoin commissions require eligible collected Live fees under an activated program.

## Can I import quick-test resources into a reward account?

No. Guest and quick-test records cannot be imported into authenticated balances or community prize inventory.
