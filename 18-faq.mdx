---
title: "Appendix B. Frequently Asked Questions"
---

## Can I withdraw dUSDC or dUSDT?

No. Demo balances are simulated units and cannot be converted into real stablecoins.

## Is an RVX Allocation already a wallet token?

No. It is a verified entitlement recorded during the pre-TGE accumulation phase. Eligible verified units convert 1:1 into RVX at distribution.

## When will airdrop and presale distribution terms be announced?

These terms are being finalized and are not announced at this stage. Presale terms will be published before participants make payment. Airdrop distribution terms will be announced before the applicable claim or conversion process begins.

## Does RAVIXO hold the assets in my current DEX wallet?

The current DEX model is self-custodial. Users keep wallet keys and sign the supported transactions themselves. Assets used in an approved transaction follow the relevant on-chain execution.

## Are all whitepaper features available now?

No. RAVIXO opens features progressively. Check the application's feature status and official announcements.

## Do referrals earn stablecoins simply for registering users?

No. Stablecoin fee commissions require an opened program, eligible collected Live platform fees and the referrer's qualification. Fixed RVX acquisition milestones are a separate allocation program.

## Is every reward figure automatically payable?

No. A reward requires an active published Campaign or Season, completion of its conditions, verification and an available reserved budget. Planned reward schedules become active only through the published program terms.

## When will RVX and the dedicated blockchain launch?

The launch schedule will be announced through official RAVIXO channels when the network and distribution requirements are met.

## Can Mine RVX convert 1:1 into RVX?

No. Mine RVX is an internal game resource. The 1:1 rule applies only to eligible verified Earned RVX Allocations in the allocation ledger.

## Can I withdraw an RVX Miner demonstration gift?

No. Demonstration records are not payable cash. Verified awards under an activated and funded Hunt or weekly Top 70 program follow that program's published eligibility, verification and payment terms.

## What is the funded Hunt chest value range?

Section 17.7 defines whole-cent values from USD 0.01 through USD 10.00. Its probability bands describe the value of an earned chest, not the probability of winning a round. Rewards require successful gameplay, server verification and available funding.

## What does the weekly Top 70 game program award?

The planned weekly distribution is USD 5,000 and 5,000 RVX across 70 qualifying positions. Top 1 receives USD 3,000 and 2,000 RVX; Top 2 receives USD 1,000 and 1,000 RVX; Top 3 receives USD 500 and 500 RVX. Section 17.6 lists every position group through Top 70. The program requires announced activation, funding and settlement terms.

## Can I withdraw RVX from the weekly game rewards now?

No. RVX is recorded internally and cannot currently be withdrawn on-chain. RAVIXO will announce withdrawal availability when the RVX network is ready. These rewards are separate from equipment-upgrade Mine RVX.

## How does a game referral become verified?

The referral appears as pending immediately after registration through your link. The invited person must pass KYC and unique-identity checks, complete 30 valid server-confirmed rounds and pass account-quality checks. This credits you 10 referral points once. Reaching 50 total valid rounds adds another 5 points once after approval. Game referrals do not require a D7/D30 waiting period or activity on a minimum number of separate days.

## Do game referral points pay RVX or automatically improve my game rank?

No. They are separate score records, capped at 15 points per verified referral. Each milestone enters the week in which its verification is completed. They do not automatically credit RVX or USD or change the game ranking formula.

## Can a new wallet or a shared network automatically disqualify a game referral?

No. DEX history can support risk review but does not automatically earn points. A shared network or device alone is insufficient to reject a person. A case needing review can provide evidence or request review, and an invalid result shows a reason.

## Does an arcade VIP title provide trading fee discounts?

No. Arcade prestige grants the disclosed game resources, cosmetics and equipment presets. It does not activate a platform trading fee tier.

## Do game invitations already earn referral commissions?

The planned game program credits the separate 30/50-round referral points after verification when it opens. It does not generate a stablecoin fee commission. Stablecoin commissions require eligible collected Live fees and qualification under an activated platform referral program. RVX ID and game referral attribution remain integration steps.

## Can I import quick-test resources into a reward account?

No. Guest and quick-test records cannot be imported into authenticated balances or community prize inventory.
