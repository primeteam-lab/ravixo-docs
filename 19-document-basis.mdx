title: "Appendix C. Whitepaper Scope and Updates"
---

This whitepaper describes RAVIXO’s ecosystem, planned RVX utility, token allocation, community programs and the RVX Miner arcade prototype. Current availability appears in the application.

Reward and fee examples describe planned programs. Published Campaign or Season terms govern eligibility, budgets and activation; examples alone do not create payment entitlements.

Presale terms will be published before payment; airdrop terms before claim or conversion. Mainnet specifications, token issuance details and launch dates will be announced as release conditions are completed.

Material updates will appear through official channels and later editions. Active Season rules remain applicable; users should check the relevant program announcement.

## Version 1.1 Update

Version 1.1, dated 22 October 2026, adds RVX Miner and aligns related product, reward and referral descriptions. RVX supply, reserve allocations and the existing conversion framework remain unchanged. Prototype settings do not create cash-payment rights or amend active funded Season terms. Any funded program requires its own published activation, budget and settlement rules.
