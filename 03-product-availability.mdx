---
title: "3. Progressive Product Availability"
---

RAVIXO is being opened progressively for users to experience and provide feedback. The initial rollout prioritizes the Dashboard, Demo Trading, available Product Quests and basic Referral functions. Data tools, leaderboards, trader profiles and supported Live DEX functions are introduced as their own launch conditions are completed.

| Area | User experience | Availability rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard and RVX ID | Account identity, progress and feature status | Open progressively in the application |
| Demo Trading | Simulated orders, PnL and practice history | Use only the instruments and modes shown as available |
| Quests and Airdrop Allocations | Published tasks, proof submission and verified reward records | Each task requires an active Campaign or Season |
| Referral | Personal link and qualified-user progress | Stablecoin commissions require eligible fee-generating Live activity |
| Data Hub, AI and Top Trader | Market context, explainable signals and performance profiles | Released in stages; coverage is displayed per feature |
| DEX Swap | Demo simulation or supported self-custodial Live execution | Live is enabled separately for supported networks and assets |
| RVX and RAVIXO Blockchain | Native fee utility, distribution and network infrastructure | Planned launch with separate technical and distribution announcements |

For early access, basic account security, order history and the reward ledger may be available inside the initial navigation areas before dedicated pages are opened. An unavailable feature is marked as In Development or Planned.
