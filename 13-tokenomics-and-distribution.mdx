## 13.1. Supply Allocation

The planned maximum supply is 580,000,000 RVX. The allocation model totals 100%.

| Allocation | Share of maximum supply | RVX units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-TGE Airdrop Allocation Program | 5% | 29,000,000 |
| Product Rewards and Season Leaderboards | 20% | 116,000,000 |
| Creator and RVX Referral Rewards | 10% | 58,000,000 |
| Reserved Presale Allocation | 10% | 58,000,000 |
| Liquidity and Market Infrastructure | 15% | 87,000,000 |
| Product Development and Security | 15% | 87,000,000 |
| Core Contributors | 10% | 58,000,000 |
| Strategic Partners | 5% | 29,000,000 |
| Treasury Reserve | 10% | 58,000,000 |
| Total | 100% | 580,000,000 |

The first three allocations form Community &amp; Growth: 35%, or 203,000,000 RVX. The pre-TGE 5% reserve covers verified Claim, onboarding, Demo, Social and eligible early-community programs. The 20% reserve funds product use and Season rankings as features open. The 10% reserve funds Creator and RVX referral rewards. Stablecoin fee commissions are accounted for separately.

For this proposed reserve design, all awards earned before TGE under the airdrop accumulation program, including eligible early Referral or Creator awards, are charged to the 29,000,000-unit pre-TGE reserve. The 20% and 10% post-TGE program reserves fund later activity and do not pay the same entitlement twice. Season announcements identify the reserve used for each award.

The presale allocation is reserved capacity, not confirmation that a sale is currently open or that all reserved units have been sold. Sale eligibility, pricing and distribution terms are announced separately before participants make payment. Unsold units remain reserved.

## 13.2. Token Distribution Announcements

Airdrop and presale distribution terms are being finalized and are not announced at this stage. Full terms will be published separately for each program.

Presale terms will be made available before participants make payment. Airdrop distribution terms will be announced before the applicable claim or conversion process begins.

The planned maximum supply and reserve allocations do not establish the amount available at launch. Actual issued and circulating supply will be reported based on verified on-chain and program records.
