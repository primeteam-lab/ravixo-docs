## 6.1. Practice and Contests

Practice Mode allows users to test supported trading strategies with simulated capital. Reward-bearing contests are separate programs with published budgets and participation rules.

When a weekly contest is opened, each eligible account receives 10,000 dUSDC. The official initial timezone is Asia/Ho\_Chi\_Minh, UTC+7. A week begins at 00:00:00 Monday and ends at 23:59:59 Sunday. The next weekly balance is issued at the following Monday reset.

Contest accounts cannot add capital during a Season. At the cutoff, pending orders are cancelled and open positions are closed using the published end-of-period mark-price method. Simulated fees, funding and realized PnL are included in the result. If a contest account is liquidated, it is not refilled during that same Season; the user may continue in Practice Mode.

Weekly balances reset, while eligible account history, badges and longer-term performance records remain. The contest rules identify supported instruments, execution assumptions, the mark-price source, minimum participation, tie-break rules and the result-verification window before entry.

## 6.2. Performance and Rankings

TraderScore prioritizes risk-adjusted performance, controlled drawdown, consistency, activity across multiple days, Profit Factor and disciplined trading. Using greater leverage does not automatically increase the score.

Weekly rankings describe the current contest. Longer-term rankings preserve history so users can distinguish one short result from sustained performance. Any published weights or eligibility thresholds remain unchanged within the active Season.

Top Trader profiles are initially based on verified Demo Trading histories. They can include realized PnL, maximum drawdown, win rate, Profit Factor, holding time, closed orders and trading style. Demo results are identified as simulated results. Users can follow and study these profiles; Copy Trading remains a later product development.
