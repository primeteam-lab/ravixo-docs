## 10.1. Qualified Active Users

A Qualified Active User, or QAU, has one eligible RVX ID, is at least seven days old, has meaningful eligible activity on at least three separate days and passes the account-quality and anti-abuse checks.

QAU D7 is the verified seven-day qualification. QAU D30 describes eligible retention through day 30. For a D30 retention percentage, the denominator includes only accounts in the relevant cohort that have reached their day-30 assessment date. A Live Qualified QAU has completed at least one eligible Live product action.

## 10.2. Five-Level Fee Commissions

| Referral level | Share of the available Referral Pool |
| --- | --- |
| F1, direct level | 55% |
| F2 | 20% |
| F3 | 12% |
| F4 | 8% |
| F5 | 5% |

These percentages apply to the Referral Pool, not to transaction volume. The planned qualification requirements are:

| Level | Qualification requirement |
| --- | --- |
| F1 | Commission recording from the first eligible QAU; commission claims enabled after at least three eligible QAUs |
| F2 | 10 QAUs, including at least three Live Qualified |
| F3 | 30 QAUs, including at least 10 Live Qualified, and at least 20% D30 retention |
| F4 | 100 QAUs, including at least 30 Live Qualified, and at least 25% D30 retention |
| F5 | 300 QAUs, including at least 100 Live Qualified, at least 30% D30 retention and TrustScore of at least 90 |

The applicable measurement window and TrustScore scale are published before activation. Unqualified shares are not compressed upward to another referrer. Their use for Community Reserve or an announced Buyback &amp; Burn budget is disclosed in the program rules.

For the self-custodial model, commission payout means a supported on-chain claim or transfer to the referrer's wallet, not withdrawal of the user's underlying DEX trading assets from a custodial exchange account. Minimum payout, settlement intervals, network costs and treatment of recorded but not yet claimable commissions are published before the program opens.

## 10.3. Swap Fee Example and Growth Mode

The planned 0.01% platform fee on a 1,000 USDC swap is 0.10 USDC. In a time-limited Growth Mode, up to 100% of the actually collected eligible platform fee can enter the Referral Pool.

| Level | Example commission in USDC |
| --- | --- |
| F1 | 0.055 |
| F2 | 0.020 |
| F3 | 0.012 |
| F4 | 0.008 |
| F5 | 0.005 |

Gas, pool fees, bridge costs and slippage are excluded from RAVIXO referral revenue. The Growth Mode period and operating budget are announced separately. Stablecoin-denominated commission examples do not prescribe a one-dollar price for RVX. If fees later settle in RVX, the quote, conversion and commission settlement method is published before that mode opens.

## 10.4. Acquisition Bonuses

The planned direct-referral milestones are one allocation unit for an eligible RVX ID, confirmed only when QAU D7 is verified; three further units at QAU D7; five further units at QAU D30; and ten further units after the QAU's first eligible Live action.

The first two milestones therefore produce four units together at verified D7. The standard maximum is 19 units per invited RVX ID. The same acquisition is not paid again through Social Share or Creator attribution. These fixed bonuses belong to the direct attribution owner, separately from the five-level fee commissions.
