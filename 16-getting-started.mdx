---
title: "16. Getting Started and Official Channels"
---

Users begin by creating an RVX ID, opening the Dashboard and choosing an available feature. They can practice Demo Trading, complete eligible quests and submit required proof. Their Reward Center shows verified allocations and task outcomes. When supported Live DEX features are available, users connect their own wallet, review transaction details and sign actions they choose to execute.

Users can share a personal referral link or create eligible original content under an opened Campaign. Participation does not require pretending that planned features are already live or that simulated results are real-money trading results.

At token distribution, eligible users follow the published Snapshot, wallet binding and claim instructions. Claimable RVX follows the applicable program terms and is separate from the Demo Wallet balance.
