Users begin by creating an RVX ID, opening the Dashboard and choosing an available feature. They can practice Demo Trading, complete eligible quests and submit required proof. Their Reward Center shows verified allocations and task outcomes. When supported Live DEX features are available, users connect their own wallet, review transaction details and sign actions they choose to execute.

Users can share a personal referral link or create eligible original content under an opened Campaign. Participation does not require pretending that planned features are already live or that simulated results are real-money trading results.

At token distribution, eligible users follow the published Snapshot, wallet binding and claim instructions. Claimable RVX follows the applicable program terms and is separate from the Demo Wallet balance.

## Exploring the Community Arcade

Where the RVX Miner prototype is offered, users can try guest play, inspect a winding campaign map and learn the equipment system. Guest and quick-test records remain separate from authenticated competition. Creating or using an RVX ID does not currently import prototype progress or activate game payouts. Check the specific game and Campaign status before assuming that an in-game gift has cash value.

When the game referral program opens, registration through a referral link creates a pending record immediately. The invited person completes KYC and 30 valid server-confirmed rounds to qualify the referrer for 10 points. Reaching 50 total valid rounds adds another 5 points after verification. Each milestone counts once, without a D7/D30 waiting period.

The planned weekly Top 70 game rewards total USD 5,000 and 5,000 RVX. Check the activation announcement, verified standings and settlement instructions before claiming. RVX is recorded internally, and on-chain withdrawal availability will be announced when the RVX network is ready. See [Section 17](/20-game-zone-and-rvx-miner).
