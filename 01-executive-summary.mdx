---
title: "1. Executive Summary"
---

RAVIXO is a crypto ecosystem connecting market data, demo trading, decentralized exchange tools, product quests and community learning. Its purpose is to help users understand market information, test strategies with simulated assets, and use supported decentralized services through a progressively expanding product experience.

RAVIXO is opening features in stages. Users should consult the status of each feature in the application: Available, In Development or Planned. A feature described in this whitepaper is not automatically available in every account or region.

The current DEX model is self-custodial: users keep control of their own wallet keys and assets. Demo balances and test-phase reward allocations are separate from real on-chain assets.

RVX is the planned fee asset for the RAVIXO ecosystem and its dedicated blockchain. The long-term direction is to settle supported ecosystem service fees in RVX and use RVX for fees on the RAVIXO network. These functions will become available as the relevant infrastructure is launched.

During the airdrop accumulation phase, verified rewards are recorded as Earned RVX Allocations. Eligible verified allocations will convert at a ratio of one allocation unit to one RVX at token distribution. Conversion establishes the total token entitlement. Distribution follows the terms announced for the applicable program.
