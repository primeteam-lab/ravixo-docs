## 9.1. Genesis Claim

Genesis Claim awards 0.3 RVX Allocation per verified eligible claim while an announced Claim Season and the user's Activity Pass are active. Claims must be at least six hours apart, with no more than four claims in a rolling 24-hour period.

Activity Pass is maintained through eligible product activity. Logging in or repeatedly pressing Claim without meaningful product use is not enough to maintain it. Each Season defines the qualifying actions, their daily limits, budget and per-account cap before participation.

Genesis Claim is part of the pre-TGE allocation program. It does not create a tradable token in the connected wallet.

## 9.2. Product Quests

Product Quests guide users through available features. Only tasks displayed as active can be completed for their published reward. The planned baseline is:

| Quest or milestone | RVX Allocation units |
| --- | --- |
| Complete an eligible RVX ID | 0.50 |
| Complete available account-security setup | 0.50 |
| Complete the product tutorial | 0.50 |
| First eligible account login, once | 0.25 |
| Complete the announced social onboarding bundle and retain it through D7 | 0.50 |
| First eligible opened and closed Demo Trading order | 0.30 |
| First successful Demo Swap | 0.20 |
| Eligible activity on three different days in the same week | 0.50 |
| Eligible activity on five different days in that week | Additional 0.50 |
| Eligible activity on seven different days in that week | Additional 1.00 |
| Eligible account retention through D7 | 1 |
| Eligible account retention through D30 | 3 |
| Eligible account retention through D90 | 8 |

D7, D30 and D90 are measured from RVX ID creation. Activity days do not need to be consecutive unless an individual quest says otherwise. An activity day requires an eligible product action or Product Quest; login and Claim alone do not count. Retention rewards require the stated activity conditions, not simply the passage of time.

## 9.3. Live Swap Milestones

The planned Live Swap mission Season lasts 30 days. Eligible executed volume can accumulate across multiple transactions.

| Highest eligible volume milestone | Reward units |
| --- | --- |
| 100 USDC | 20 |
| 1,000 USDC | 60 |
| 10,000 USDC | 200 |
| More than 50,000 USDC | 500 |

Only the highest milestone is paid. If a lower milestone has already been credited, reaching a higher one credits the difference rather than a second full milestone payment. Returning for eligible swaps in weeks 2, 3 and 4 can add 10, 10 and 20 units respectively.

The planned maximum is 540 units per person per Season: a 500-unit volume milestone plus 40 retention units. A separately announced Top DEX prize is a different reward category. All rewards must remain within the reserved Season budget.

## 9.4. Campaign Bonuses

An announced Campaign may offer these additional bonuses:

- Complete all eight verified short community tasks in Section 11.2: 50 additional allocation units.

- Reach 10,000 USDC of eligible Live Swap volume: the same 200-unit milestone in Section 9.3, not a duplicate payment.

- Invite three distinct eligible friends who reach verified QAU D7: 80 allocation units, once per eligible referrer in that Campaign.

The planned 80-unit Campaign bonus is a separate group milestone and may stack with the standard direct-referral acquisition bonuses if the Campaign expressly permits it. It does not increase the 19-unit standard acquisition cap for an individual invited account. Each invited account counts once for that Campaign; every award remains subject to the announced budget and account cap.

## 9.5. Reward Center and Season Rules

Reward Center displays RXP, Earned RVX Allocations, task status, Activity Pass, eligible score records and upcoming milestones. Stablecoin commission records and LiveScore appear only for opened programs that use them.

Each active Season publishes its start and end time, timezone, reward budget, participation criteria, score or ranking method, per-account limits, stacking rules, proof requirements, review window, distribution schedule and treatment of unclaimed or unused rewards. Published rules are not changed retrospectively within an active Season.

User-visible task outcomes include the reason for rejection or ineligibility and a route to request review. Anti-abuse decisions should not rely solely on a shared household network; the program evaluates relevant ownership and activity evidence without publicly exposing detection methods.
