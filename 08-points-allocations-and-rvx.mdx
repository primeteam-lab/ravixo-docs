title: "8. RXP, RVX Allocations and RVX"
---

RAVIXO separates three kinds of rewards and account information.

| Record or asset | Meaning | Transferability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RXP | Product experience points used for progress and activity recognition | Non-transferable account points |
| Earned RVX Allocation | Verified pre-TGE entitlement recorded in the reward ledger | Not an issued token; no trading or withdrawal during the accumulation phase |
| RVX | Planned native asset of the RAVIXO blockchain and ecosystem fee utility | On-chain use begins after issuance under the applicable network and distribution terms |

Stablecoin referral commissions are a separate category. They arise only from eligible, actually collected Live platform fees under an opened commission program. Demo trading and registration alone do not generate stablecoin commissions.

## 8.1. Airdrop Accumulation and 1:1 Conversion

Eligible verified Earned RVX Allocations will convert 1:1 into RVX at distribution. For example, 1,000 eligible allocation units correspond to a total entitlement of 1,000 RVX.

The 1:1 ratio defines the total RVX entitlement; it does not establish a distribution date or a one-dollar value for RVX. Distribution arrangements will be announced separately for the applicable program.

Tasks move through Submitted, Under Review and Verified states. Only verified eligible results create Earned RVX Allocations. These are task-processing states, not temporary token types.

Before Snapshot, RAVIXO publishes the cutoff time, eligible programs, account-to-wallet binding procedure, verification deadlines and the claim process. A user can review their verified total and submit a correction request within the announced review window. Snapshot fixes the approved entitlement dataset; token distribution follows the published program terms.

Genesis Claim ends at the announced Snapshot or the applicable budget limit. A token-launch delay does not create an automatic new allocation, change the 1:1 entitlement ratio or make an unverified task eligible. Updated distribution dates are communicated through official channels. Existing rights under published Season terms are respected.
