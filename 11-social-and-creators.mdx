## 11.1. Standard Social Quests

| Proposed task | RVX Allocation units |
| --- | --- |
| Follow the official X account, once | 0.20 |
| Join the official Telegram Channel, once | 0.20 |
| Join the official Telegram Group, once | 0.20 |
| Subscribe to the official YouTube channel, once | 0.20 |
| Repost the official pinned Campaign post | 1.00 |
| Share an eligible regular official post | 0.50 |
| Quote Post with meaningful original commentary | 0.80 |
| Eligible cross-platform share with original context | 1.50 |

The proposed standard plan opens two or three paid Share Campaigns each week, with a combined Base Reward cap of approximately three units per week. One original eligible content unit can receive a Base Reward in a Campaign. Approved distribution of that content on additional platforms can receive 50% and 25% of the first-platform Base Reward, subject to the same combined weekly cap. The first platform receives 100%.

Content must remain public for at least seven days. It must identify rewarded Campaign participation and follow the publishing platform's applicable rules. Fake clicks, views and accounts do not qualify.

Proposed verified Reach Bonus milestones are 2 units at 1,000 reach, 5 at 5,000, 15 at 20,000 and 50 at 100,000. This draft applies the highest verified milestone only: advancing after an earlier payment produces a top-up to the higher amount, not the sum of all milestone values. Reach definitions, evidence and the assessment window are published per Campaign.

Impact milestones use the same one-plus-three-plus-five-plus-ten acquisition structure in Section 10.4, with one combined 19-unit cap per acquired account across Referral, Social Share and Creator channels.

## 11.2. Eight Short Community Tasks

| Platform | Original short post | Original short video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reddit | 8 | 25 |
| Facebook | 6 | 30 |
| Instagram | 6 | 35 |
| TikTok | 5 | 40 |

These are eight separate tasks when their Campaign is opened. Completing all eight earns 155 allocation units before the separate 50-unit completion bonus, for a total of 205 verified units.

A short post uses the participant's own wording, includes RAVIXO, the Campaign hashtag and their personal referral link. An original 10-20 second clip includes RAVIXO in text or speech, the Campaign hashtag and the referral link. Where direct links are unavailable, the announced bio link, QR code, referral code or pinned-comment method can be used.

Participants submit public URLs for content and attribution review. These tasks are outside the standard three-unit Share Base Reward cap. A specific content item receives one eligible content-reward category; it cannot also receive a second Share or Creator content payment for the same work. Distinct original tasks must meet the Campaign's originality rules.

## 11.3. Creator Program

Creators can participate through original writing, infographics, analysis, short videos, long tutorials and localized content on approved platforms.

| Content category | Proposed base reward units | Proposed performance reward units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original post or thread | 3-10 | 5-50 |
| Infographic or data analysis | 10-30 | 20-100 |
| Short video | 20-80 | 50-300 |
| Long video or detailed tutorial | 80-300 | 100-1,000 |
| Localized content | 30-200 | Additional verified-QAU rewards as announced |
| Qualified-user Campaign | 200-2,000 | Eligible Creator Season prizes |

Creator content remains public for at least 30 days. A content item does not receive both a Social Share content reward and a Creator content reward; the higher eligible category is used. Performance ranges are not automatic payments for uploading content. The Campaign defines evaluation criteria, budget and caps.

The proposed CreatorScore weights are 25% content quality, 20% verified reach, 30% qualified users, 15% retention and 10% approved multi-platform distribution. Score normalization and tie-break rules are disclosed before the Season.

## 11.4. Campaign Links and Attribution

Campaign Links identify content, platform and eligible conversion events. An illustrative format is ravixo.io/c/CREATOR123/YOUTUBE045; this example is not a claim that the route is already deployed.

The referral owner is assigned when an eligible RVX ID is first created. Campaign attribution measures the performance of content and does not silently replace the account's referral owner. A person generates one acquisition reward record, not repeated payments through several attribution channels.

Relevant records may include unique visits, onboarding, qualified-user milestones, eligible product activity and platform fees. Data handling and supported evidence methods are explained in the application and the applicable privacy and Campaign rules. False views, copied content, self-referrals, controlled account clusters and artificial volume are excluded.
