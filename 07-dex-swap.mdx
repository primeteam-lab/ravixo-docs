## 7.1. Demo Swap

Demo Swap simulates tokens, routes, pools, price impact, slippage and gas. It creates no blockchain transaction and charges no real network fee. It is intended to make execution mechanics understandable before using a supported Live route.

## 7.2. Self-Custodial Live Swap

Supported Live Swap uses real assets in the user's connected wallet. The user reviews the quote and signs the required transaction. Smart Router is designed to compare the estimated net outcome across supported routes, including pool fees, network fees, price impact, slippage and bridge costs where applicable.

The planned RAVIXO platform fee is 0.01% of eligible swap volume. For example, a 1,000 USDC swap has a platform fee of 0.10 USDC. This platform fee is separate from pool/LP fees, gas, bridge costs and execution price effects.

Before confirmation, the interface displays the estimated output, minimum received amount and separate cost components. The actual result depends on transaction execution and the available route. Supported networks, assets, routing integrations and limits are disclosed as they become available.

A separate newcomer pool can allocate 2,000 units per week using the newcomer Trading distribution in Section 6.3. The same weekly activity receives only the higher general or newcomer prize.

Top DEX Swap Volume uses eligible executed volume. LiveScore-based Season pools are separate programs that also consider retention, activity across multiple days and contribution quality. Their budgets and stacking rules are identified individually rather than presented as one ranking method.

Self-generated volume, duplicate ownership and flagged activity are excluded. Pre-TGE rewards are recorded as verified allocations; post-TGE settlement follows the published token distribution rules.
