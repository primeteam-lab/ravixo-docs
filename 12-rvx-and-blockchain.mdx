## 12.1. Planned Fee Utility

RVX is planned to become the payment asset for supported services across the RAVIXO ecosystem. The long-term fee gateway direction is to quote and settle applicable ecosystem service fees using RVX after the token and payment infrastructure are available.

RVX is also planned as the native fee asset of the dedicated RAVIXO blockchain. This connects the asset's practical use to network transactions and supported ecosystem activity. It does not make every external-chain gas fee payable in RVX: external networks retain their own fee requirements unless a supported conversion or routing service is expressly introduced.

The interface will state the fee amount, payment asset, pricing method and estimated total cost before a supported RVX-paid action.

## 12.2. RAVIXO Blockchain Direction

RAVIXO intends to build and operate its own blockchain infrastructure to control the network environment, ecosystem integrations and network fee design. Mainnet in this document means the production RAVIXO blockchain, rather than simply deploying an application on another network.

The network remains a planned development. Its consensus design, validator model, chain ID, supported wallet standards, explorer, security testing, fee parameters and any cross-chain connections are published in a technical release before production use. This whitepaper does not claim a completed Mainnet deployment, a completed audit or a specific unannounced consensus implementation.

RVX issuance and claim infrastructure must be coordinated with a supported production network. Any change to the launch sequence is explained before distribution. The proposed supply model does not assume extra inflation outside the maximum supply in Section 13.
