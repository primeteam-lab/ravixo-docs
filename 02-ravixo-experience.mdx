---
title: "2. The RAVIXO Experience"
---

Crypto users often move between separate tools to inspect tokens, interpret data, test a trading idea, execute a decentralized swap and learn from others. RAVIXO brings these activities into one coordinated experience.

The product is built around five principles:

- Understand the information: combine relevant market, on-chain, DEX, derivatives, technical and social data.

- Test before committing: use simulated assets to practice and evaluate trading behavior.

- Keep wallet control: use supported DEX functions from a user-controlled wallet.

- Learn from evidence: study transparent demo histories, performance profiles and community content.

- Reward meaningful contribution: recognize verified product use, retention, referrals and original content within announced budgets.

RAVIXO serves learners, active crypto users, traders who want to test ideas, and creators who explain the ecosystem to their communities. The same account can participate in several roles, subject to the rules of the applicable program.
