## 14.1. Revenue and Operating Resources

RAVIXO's initial economic model centers on the planned DEX platform fee, time-limited Growth Mode and separately approved operating resources. Network fees on the future RAVIXO blockchain are a separate fee category; their allocation is disclosed in the technical network parameters.

If Growth Mode routes all eligible DEX platform fees to referrals, that same fee cannot simultaneously fund operations or buybacks. Its duration requires a separately approved operating budget. Operating resources may come from approved treasury resources, completed and announced fundraising, or other opened revenue services. A token reserve is not presented as an existing cash balance.

Potential later revenue services include supported advanced data tools and future trading features. They require their own launch notice, pricing and availability conditions.

## 14.2. Planned Futures Fee Framework

Futures is a planned addition to the ecosystem. The following fee tiers describe its planned framework; the service and fee schedule will be activated through a separate launch announcement.

| Tier | Eligible 30-day volume | Maker | Taker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regular | Below 100,000 USDC | 0.020% | 0.050% |
| VIP 1 | At least 100,000 and below 1,000,000 USDC | 0.018% | 0.045% |
| VIP 2 | At least 1,000,000 and up to 10,000,000 USDC | 0.015% | 0.040% |
| VIP 3 | Above 10,000,000 USDC | 0.010% | 0.035% |

Market-maker terms, execution, custody and margin mechanics are disclosed separately before launch. The planned split of actually collected eligible Futures platform fees is 60% Referral Pool, 25% operations and security, and 15% Buyback &amp; Burn. The final fee allocation and activation date will be published before the Futures service opens.

For illustration, 100,000 USDC of Taker volume at 0.050% generates 50 USDC in platform fees: 30 for the Referral Pool, 12.50 for operations and security, and 7.50 for the planned buyback budget. Network or third-party costs are not assumed to be included in that revenue figure.

## 14.3. Buyback &amp; Burn

Buyback &amp; Burn uses only an announced funded budget. The program identifies the revenue source, amount approved, execution period and verifiable transactions. Repurchased RVX counts as burned only after the permanent on-chain burn is recorded; tokens merely held in a treasury wallet are not burned.

The dashboard should distinguish collected fees, amounts allocated to referrals or operations, approved buyback funds, RVX purchased and RVX permanently burned. Buybacks are not automatically assumed for every product, every transaction or every market condition.
