Official English Whitepaper | Version 1.1 | 23 October 2026

RAVIXO connects crypto market data, demo trading, decentralized exchange tools, community learning and arcade experiences in one ecosystem. Users can explore available features, practice with simulated assets and complete eligible activities to earn verified RVX Allocations under the applicable program rules. RAVIXO is opening progressively, with current availability shown in the application.

RVX fee payments, the dedicated RAVIXO blockchain and token distribution form part of the development roadmap. The reward schedules, fee models and technical directions in this whitepaper describe the planned framework. Each program becomes active through its published Campaign or Season terms. Airdrop and presale distribution terms are being finalized and will be announced separately before the applicable payment, claim or conversion process.

RVX Miner is available as a separate playable prototype with campaign maps, earned upgrades and weekly internal-resource competition. Hunt is designed to award real USD gifts of USD 0.05-10.00 after you deliver two distinct keys and the unlocked gift in one round, subject to verification and an open, allocated prize fund. Mine RVX remains an internal game resource, separate from real USD rewards, transferable RVX and verified token allocations. RVX ID integration, game referral attribution and automatic external payments remain development steps. See [Section 17](/20-game-zone-and-rvx-miner) for the full rules and [the game walkthrough](https://notebooklm.link.google/0rBPwWqhZOhF) for a visual explanation.
