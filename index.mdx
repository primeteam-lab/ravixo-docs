Explore Data. Practice Strategies. Grow Together.

English Whitepaper | Review Draft v0.3 | 3 July 2025

This draft describes the progressively opening RAVIXO ecosystem and its development direction. The RVX fee utility, dedicated blockchain and token launch are planned developments. Token distribution terms are being finalized and will be announced separately for each program. Additional program clarifications in this edition are proposed for review; they are not evidence of deployed contracts or already active campaigns. Availability and the terms of each active Season are published in the application and official announcements.
