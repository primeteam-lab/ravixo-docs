---
title: "15. Product Roadmap"
---

The roadmap follows product and network readiness milestones. Features become usable only when the application and official announcements identify them as open.

| Stage | Main focus | User-facing release conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community Rollout | Dashboard, Demo Trading, active Product Quests and basic Referral | Usable account experience, simulated order history, verified reward ledger and published active Campaign budgets |
| Product Expansion | Data Hub, explainable AI tools, Demo Swap, rankings and trader profiles | Supported data coverage, documented scoring, reliable simulation and result verification |
| Supported Live DEX | Self-custodial swaps, routing, eligible Live rewards and fee commissions | Supported networks and assets, quote and cost display, wallet signing and announced program settlement rules |
| RVX and RAVIXO Mainnet | Dedicated blockchain, RVX issuance, 1:1 distribution and fee-payment utility | Published network details, production readiness, Snapshot results, claim instructions and program distribution terms |
| Ecosystem Expansion | Additional networks and routes, advanced data services, spot order-book tools, Copy Trading and possible Futures | Product-specific architecture, security, execution, fee and user-protection disclosures before each launch |

The planned Futures direction considers leverage introduced gradually from x1. Any higher levels, including a possible maximum of x125, depend on separate product decisions and controls. Neither Futures nor Copy Trading is described as an already opened service by this roadmap.

Current feature status takes precedence over a broad roadmap label. Network launch, token issuance, market availability and individual feature activation are announced as distinct events when needed.
